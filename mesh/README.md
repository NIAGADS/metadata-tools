# MeSH Annotation of Legacy DSS Studies and Datasets

This folder contains the files and scripts used to back-annotate legacy DSS
studies and datasets with subject headings from the Medical Subject Headings
(MeSH) vocabulary. The annotations support FAIR data principles by making
existing study and dataset records easier to find, access, integrate, and
reuse through consistent, machine-readable subject terms. MeSH terms are
derived by querying publications affiliated with the studies and extracting
the MeSH terms assigned to those publications. The extracted terms are then
reviewed and filtered to exclude broad, geographic, administrative, and other
non-informative concepts that do not add value for NIAGADS cataloging and
discovery.

## Publications MeSH Snapshots

`publications-MeSH-annotations-snapshot.txt` contains a snapshot of MeSH terms NIAGADS authored or NIAGADS affiliated
publications that are not necessarily affiliated with DSS-hosted data. This review is intended
as an extension to the legacy documentation to help provide more comprehensive list of recommended
headings for incoming data submissions.  These are generated from parsing internal cache of
publication metadata generated by the revised NIAGADS main site `/api/publication` route.  

> Current Snapshot: 2026-10-07

## Annotation files and interpretation

`study-MeSH-annotations.txt` and `dataset-MeSH-annotations.txt` contain
accession, MeSH term, and MeSH CURIE pairs for DSS studies and datasets. The
terms are inferred from affiliated publications and should be treated as
cataloging recommendations rather than a complete description of the data.
The absence of a term does not necessarily mean that a study or dataset has
no relevant subject matter; it may reflect missing publication links or
publications without MeSH headings.

## MeSH term exclusion criteria

MeSH terms extracted from the publication, study, and dataset files were
identified with assistance from ChatGPT (OpenAI) for their usefulness in NIAGADS topic
cataloging, search, and indexing. Exclusion decisions were based on meaning, not frequency.

Recommendations are found in `recommended_term_exclusions.txt`

Terms were excluded when they described:

* **Geographic locations:** countries, regions, states, and cities that identify study location rather than scientific content.
* **Broad or generic concepts:** terms such as Research, Biology, Medicine, Disease, and Humans that provide little distinction within the NIAGADS catalog.
* **Generic research activities or descriptors:** terms such as Data Analysis, Research Design, Sample Size, and Time Factors that do not identify a specific scientific method or data type.
* **Organizational or administrative context:** institutions, professional societies, conferences, personnel, and workplace concepts that do not describe the scientific content.

Terms were retained when they could identify a phenotype, population, demographic characteristic, biological entity or process, tissue, organism, exposure, data type, specific method, or repository function.

Exclusions apply to derived topic keywords; original MeSH annotations should be preserved.
